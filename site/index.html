Split tunneling

Choose which apps and sites go through the VPN and which go direct. Each mode keeps its own app list.

No leaks

Kill switch blocks the internet if the VPN drops. DNS and IPv6 stay inside the tunnel, local ports are password-protected.

Quick Settings tile, Pause and Disconnect buttons in the notification, Always-on VPN. Keeps running after you swipe the app away.
